## New

- Web Apps turns a website into a Mac app with its own window and separate data. The app is saved in your Applications folder.
- Menu bar monitoring can show every enabled metric in one item, rotating through them. Click the item to see them all.
- You can copy a Hermes message.

## Improvements

- Hermes can update its runtime to 0.23.2. Updating from 0.23.1 keeps your sessions, keys and memory, and does not change Hermes settings you already set.
- My List is now part of Collections.
- The developer map lists Hong Kong and Taiwan as part of China.
- A security scan keeps its status when you leave the scan tab. A stopped scan is announced even from another tab.
- Confirming text with an input method no longer creates a web app.

## Fixes

- A web app build that fails no longer leaves a broken app behind.
- The Articles section has been removed.
